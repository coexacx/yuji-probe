Newly opened pale green leaves on a few naturally irregular trees at the sides, sparse small wildflowers near the shore; remnants of snow only high up. Gentle daylight under high thin clouds. Photographic realism, restrained color, genuine-looking weathered rock, believable foliage, subtle ripples and imperfect reflections, no surreal sharpness or excessive HDR. Keep the middle quiet and spacious so text cards can overlay it. No people, buildings, boats, text, logos, watermark, border, collage, fantasy, illustration, painted shapes, fake bokeh, neon colors or glow. Render the scene only, not a dashboard.
